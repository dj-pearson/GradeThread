# SEO landscape: reseller software + clothing condition grading

Date: 2026-09-08. Client: gradethread.com. Method: 20 WebSearch queries (Google-backed
results), 6 Brave SERP fetches, ~35 page fetches. Google AI Overviews could not be fetched
directly (Google returns an error page to the fetcher); Brave's answer box was used as the
only observable AI-answer proxy. Bing via the fetcher only honors the first keyword, so it
was discarded. The session's search budget ran out after query 20, so the remaining
signals come from direct page fetches.

Headline: gradethread.com did NOT appear in the top results for ANY of the 19 non-branded
queries, including the four condition/grading queries where it has 108 /grading/ pages,
74 /care/ pages, 51 /condition-index/ pages and a 240-post blog (846 URLs in sitemap.xml).
It only surfaces on its own brand query. The content exists; the rankings do not.

## 3. Authority signals (no paid tools)

- Listicle inclusion: Vendoo, List Perfectly, Crosslist and Nifty appear in every
  third-party ranking fetched (threecolts.com, voolist.com, flowlister.com, closo.co,
  resylr.com, 3dsellers.com). Flyp and PrimeLister appear in about half. Reeva and OneShop
  only in older posts. GradeThread appears in none.
- Review sites: Capterra AU lists Vendoo at 4.7/5 on 3 votes. G2 and Product Hunt pages
  for Vendoo returned 403/404 to the fetcher; no evidence of a meaningful G2 presence for
  any of the eleven.
- Referral/affiliate: List Perfectly runs the strongest program (20% lifetime, no sales
  team, codes on Reddit r/referralcodes). Vendoo's /affiliate 404s but its help center
  points to YouTube tutorials; Flyp leans on creator testimonial videos. Reseller
  YouTubers routinely carry Vendoo / List Perfectly / Crosslist codes; that is the main
  backlink and brand-search driver for the category.
- Press: eBay's innovation.ebayinc.com and TechCrunch cover eBay's own "magical listing
  tool"; ecommercebytes.com and valueaddedresource.net cover eBay condition-policy
  changes. No press coverage for any crosslister surfaced in the 2026 SERPs.
- Owned communities: Listing Party (List Perfectly), Flyp Facebook group, Vendoo webinars.
- App-store listings rank on their own (Poshwatch, SwiftSeller, PrimeLister on "poshmark
  bot"; Size AI on "how to measure clothing for ebay"). GradeThread's Firefox add-on
  listing already ranks on the brand query; the iOS listing could be pushed onto
  measurement / grading queries with a keyworded title and subtitle.
