# Reseller inventory / bookkeeping / analytics / sourcing / comps / shipping tools

Competitive research cluster for the FlipDesk "all-in-one reseller toolkit" positioning.
Date: 2026-09-08. External research only; nothing in the repo was touched.

Method: 24 web searches (the session's shared search budget ran out at that point), 34 page
fetches. Reddit (old.reddit.com, www.reddit.com, and a jina proxy) is blocked from this
environment, so Reddit sentiment below comes from third-party pages that quote Reddit, plus
the eBay Community forum, Trustpilot, and App Store review pages, which were fetched
directly. Anything I could not confirm on a fetched page is marked (unverified).

Prices are US dollars, monthly unless stated.

## 1. My Reseller Genie

### Overview
Web-only bookkeeping + inventory app built for thrift/clothing resellers (Poshmark, eBay,
Mercari focus). Positioned as "Accounting Software for Resellers". Not a listing tool.

### Features
- Inventory with bin/location, bulk upload (spreadsheet), per-item COGS.
- Sales + expense tracking across platforms, P&L, cash OR accrual inventory method
  ("both cash inventory and accrual inventory methods", FAQ).
- Sales analytics by department/category/brand (gross profit, ASP, sell-through) on
  Premium and up.
- Marketplace sync: "My Reseller Genie currently has Poshmark, eBay, and Mercari
  integration included in the Ultimate plan." (FAQ) Everything else is manual/CSV.
- Bank/PayPal expense import and mileage tracking (per resellaio comparison page, unverified
  on MRG's own site).
- Consignment and "bulk matching" on Ultimate.
- No native mobile app: "a web app that can be accessed on any internet-enabled device"
  with a note that larger screens are recommended.

### Pricing
- Basic $9.99, Premium $14.99 (adds analytics), Ultimate $19.99 (adds marketplace
  integrations, consignment, bulk matching). Source: resellaio.app comparison page,
  "pricing accurate as of July 2026". MRG's own /pricing page renders client-side and
  did not return numbers to the fetcher, so the tier prices are (unverified) against
  the vendor page itself.
- 14-day money-back guarantee.

### Sentiment
- theecommercemom review cons: "difficult to upload existing inventory if you do not have
  excel", "no field to enter sourcing location", "other expenses have to be manually
  entered", web-only, requires manual entry of individual items.
  https://theecommercemom.com/my-reseller-genie-review/
- Same review pros: "detailed analytics by department, category, brand", "easy to
  generate profit and loss statements".
- Competitor framing (resellaio.app): MRG "records sales after they happen" rather than
  pre-sale fee calculation; "doesn't offer marketplace price lookup"; tax features are
  "US-specific". https://resellaio.app/vs/my-reseller-genie
- eBay Community "Bookkeeping and inventory software recommendations" (1 yr old): nobody in
  a 13-reply thread named MRG; the split was Seller Ledger (3), spreadsheets (5), Quicken,
  Xero, Sixbit. https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Bookkeeping-and-inventory-software-recommendations/td-p/34872392
- A user "tried My Reseller Genie for a month and found it fantastic, but preferred
  Flipwise's visual interface." (resylr.com roundup, secondhand, unverified)

## 2. Flipwise (flipwise.app)

### Overview
eBay-first inventory, accounting and automation suite "built by resellers for resellers".
Auto-syncs sales, fees and shipping from eBay; other channels are logged by hand.

### Features
- eBay sync (sales, fees, shipping) with two-year historical backfill.
- Inventory aging, days-to-sell, "death pile" (unlisted inventory) tracking.
- Automatic relisting (end + relist before auto-renew) and percentage-based repricing on
  each relist; delayed promotion.
- Expense ledger with Schedule C categories; income statement across marketplaces;
  "Supports cash or accrual"; TurboTax-compatible export.
- Mileage: "Track your sourcing miles and roll them into your Schedule C at tax time."
- Sale and location alerts; error detection / auto-reconciliation.
- Non-eBay: "Record Amazon, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or offline sales right alongside
  eBay." (manual entry, no API sync)

### Pricing (flipwise.app/pricing, fetched)
- Store size = "Total active listings + sold over the last 30 days."
- Free: up to 24 listings (new members only), 7-day money-back guarantee.
- Standard: $19.99/mo for 100-499 listings. (The Closo review also lists a ~$9.99 tier
  under 100 listings and calls $19.99 "Growth", so the tier names on the public page
  appear to have changed in 2026; treat tier names as unverified.)
- Enterprise: 25,000+ listings, custom.
- An eBay forum poster rejected Flipwise "due to cost ($40/month)" for his store size,
  which suggests the 500-5,000 listing tiers land around $40. (unverified)

### Sentiment
- Testimonials on the homepage: "Using flipwise for taxes, my accountant never saw a
  first-year business owner do as good a job as I did." (@mnatmosphere33);
  "With over 50,000 eBay listings, Flipwise will make my selling experience much easier."
  (@victorpinto7555) https://flipwise.app/
- Closo review cons: "eBay-centric: Limited integration with Poshmark, Depop, Vinted";
  mobile "can feel a bit cramped"; sync delay "A $200 sale wasn't showing up...appeared
  four hours later"; "Ghost inventory" when manual updates are forgotten.
  Pro quote: "I realized I was losing money on heavy winter coats because I wasn't
  accounting for the shipping variance correctly."
  https://closo.co/blogs/blog/is-flipwise-the-inventory-savior-we-were-promised-an-honest-review
- eBay Community, knifeaholic (1 yr): considered leaving spreadsheets, "rejected Flipwise
  due to cost ($40/month)".
  https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Questions-for-those-who-use-Seller-Ledger/td-p/34850278/

## 4. Vendoo Analytics (analytics part only)

### Overview
Vendoo is a crosslister. "Profit Tracking & Business Analytics" ships on every paid plan.

### Features (analytics only)
- Profit after marketplace fees, shipping and item cost; revenue by marketplace; top
  categories and brands; "Individual user analytics" line on the plan chart.
- No tax document prep: Vendoo "doesn't include features that help prepare tax
  documents" (nifty.ai review). No mileage, no bank feed, no Schedule C.
- Sale detection is what feeds it; users report detection misses (see quotes).

### Pricing (vendoo.co/pricing, fetched 2026-09)
- Free $0 (5 items). Starter $14.99 ($12.49 annual), Growth $29.99 ($24.99 annual, adds
  AI listing enhancement, bulk actions, 300 PhotoRoom removals), Pro $59.99 ($49.99
  annual, 1,500 removals), Enterprise custom (>1,000 new listings/mo). All paid plans:
  unlimited items. Add-ons (bulk delist/relist, importing) sold separately.

### Sentiment
- Trustpilot: 4.2/5 from 237 reviews. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vendoo.co
  - "I love the auto sale detection and delist feature. You never have to worry about
    double selling an item." (Rhonda, 5 stars, 2026-08-29)
  - "doesn't play nicely with Mercari and it's my least sales platform." (ERR, 4 stars)
  - "I've been banned from Poshmark because Vendoo didn't update the quantity of an
    item." (Adam Greene, 1 star, 2026-07-23)
  - "It is garbage. So often it is not able to correctly pull items from eBay."
    (Templeton Peck, 1 star)
  - "I tried the 'Growth' package and spent three hours attempting to import my items...
    it was a headache." (Ricardo, 1 star, 2026-08-01)
- nifty.ai review, Reddit quote (2025-02-26): "The tool does not automatically delist
  items when they're sold. Users must manually remove items". (Contradicts the Trustpilot
  praise; sale detection reliability is clearly variable.)
  https://nifty.ai/post/vendoo-review
- Verdict in nifty.ai: "worth it for casual sellers managing small inventories, but falls
  short for serious and part-time resellers who need advanced, reliable automation and
  inventory analytics."

## 5. List Perfectly Insights (analytics part only)

### Overview
Crosslister with a catalog and a "sales and analytics dashboard" gated by plan.

### Features
- "Access to sales and analytics dashboard to view insights from detected sales or
  manually added sales" (plan chart). Detection is by delist/sale sync, so undetected
  sales must be added by hand.
- Pricing tool on all plans = "Google Lens & eBay Lookup" (a lookup shortcut, not
  aggregated comps).
- No tax export, mileage, or expenses on the plan chart.

### Pricing (listperfectly.com/pricing, fetched)
- Simple $29 (no analytics), Business $49 (analytics), Pro $69, Pro Plus $99+.
- Third-party summaries say analytics starts at Pro $69; the vendor chart fetched today
  shows it starting at Business $49. Use $49.

### Sentiment
- selleraider.com framing: lower tiers "exclude essential features like bulk importing,
  syncing, and analytics" so most sellers end up at $69.
  https://selleraider.com/list-perfectly-pricing/
- No direct reseller quotes about the analytics module found; it is a secondary feature.

## 6. Terapeak (eBay Product Research)

### Overview
eBay's built-in sold-data research, free to all Seller Hub sellers since April 2021,
rebranded "Product Research" in Seller Hub; now also inside the eBay mobile app
("Selling Tab > Seller Tools").

### Features
- Up to 3 years of eBay sold data (vs 90 days in plain search); filters for 7/30/90 days,
  last year, custom range, condition, price band, site (com/co.uk/de).
- Sell-through = (Total Sold / Total Active) x 100. Closo's rule of thumb: ">100% STR:
  High demand; buy immediately", "Under 20% STR: High competition; item may sit for
  months".
- Mobile: "scan barcodes in the aisle of Goodwill".
- eBay-only data. No Poshmark, Mercari, Depop. No profit-after-fees for a purchase
  decision; no image search on the research tab (Closo article).
- No public API: an eBay dev-forum thread asks "Get data of terapeak research using API";
  there is none. https://community.ebay.com/t5/Traditional-APIs-Search/Get-data-of-terapeak-research-using-API/m-p/34521356

### Pricing
- Free with any eBay seller account. (Historically a paid Store add-on.)

### Sentiment
- Capterra reviews are thin and mostly generic (page 2: "Selling is not the best still
  like other avenues better." Kelly R., 4 stars, Jan 2023).
  https://www.capterra.com/p/181854/Terapeak/reviews/?page=2
- Positioning by competitors: WorthPoint reviewers point to Terapeak as the free
  alternative for modern goods; marketplaceiq argues eBay-only comps under-price
  apparel: "If you're sourcing apparel, shoes, or modern jewelry and you only check eBay,
  you're leaving real money on the floor." https://marketplaceiq.app/blog/poshmark-sold-price-checker

## 7. WorthPoint

### Overview
Paid price-guide database of past auction/eBay sales (antiques, collectibles, art),
plus Marks & Logos library. Not a modern-apparel tool.

### Features
- Historical sold archive, unlimited searches, marks library; Pro adds deeper archive
  and export. Business tier per-seat.
- Coverage gap: "Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, and Etsy have become
  major sales channels. WorthPoint doesn't meaningfully aggregate sold data from any of
  them." "If you sell contemporary clothing, sneakers, electronics, toys, video games,
  or general retail merchandise...WorthPoint's database won't help much."
  https://underpricedai.com/blog/worthpoint-review

### Pricing
- underpricedai: Standard $29.99/mo or $249.99/yr; Pro $59.99/mo or $599.99/yr; Team
  custom.
- britannicauctions (2026): monthly $28.99 / $37.99 / $46.99; annual $259.99 / $399.99 /
  $449.99; Business from $36.83/user/mo annual. The two sources disagree; WorthPoint has
  repriced during 2026 (unverified which is current).

### Sentiment
- "the requirement to already know what an item is before you can research it is a
  dealbreaker for generalist pickers" (underpricedai alternatives article, paraphrased by
  search summary). https://underpricedai.com/blog/worthpoint-alternative
- "It fits full-time resellers and dealers who price items daily, and at that volume,
  $30 a month pays for itself fast." (bestantiqueapps, unverified)

## 9. Sellhound

### Overview
- 2019: "virtual listing assistant" (photograph item, humans+AI return title,
  description, specifics, price, shipping) and a free "Fetch Engine" price search across
  "5 - 10 marketplaces", focused on fashion "branded from the last 40 years" because
  "fashion makes up over half of all secondhand item traded online". Founder Suzanne
  Wouk. Per-listing fee "$1.44 - $2 fee per listing (depending on the pack)", later
  $2.00-$2.50. https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2019/12/07/sellhound-aims-to-take-the-tedium-out-of-online-selling/
- 2026: sellhound.com is now an AI product-photography service (studio/lifestyle/model
  shots from one photo), $19 / $49 / $129 per month for 50 / 150 / 500 "photoshoots".
  The listing-assistant and comps products are gone from the site. https://sellhound.com/
- Not to be confused with "SellerHound" (indie iOS app by Eric Larson, $12.99/mo, eBay
  analytics + COGS + mileage; 5.0 from 2 ratings; review: "Really solid eBay app for
  keeping track of my cost of goods and mileage!").
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sellerhound-ebay-listing-tool/id6751864733

### Takeaway
The fashion-focused per-listing assistant did not survive as a business; the pivot to
photo generation is a signal that "do my listing for $2" had weak unit economics.

## 11. Poshmark / eBay "sold comps" tools

### What exists
- Manual: Poshmark "Sold" filter (mobile "filter is easy to miss - wrong filter combos
  return zero comps when there are dozens"); eBay sold filter (90 days) / Terapeak
  (3 yrs).
- Scrapers: Apify "Poshmark Sold Listings Scraper" and "poshmark-sold-comps-scraper",
  about $2.00 per 1K items, CSV/JSON. https://apify.com/caffein.dev/poshmark-sold-listings
- Poshwatch: trending products, avg days to sell, saturation, STR, avg price (Poshmark
  only). (unverified pricing)
- MarketplaceIQ: cross-platform comps, "14-day Plus trial on every signup".
- Link generators: underpriced.app "Sold Comps Tool 2026: eBay, Mercari & Poshmark
  Links" (free), ResellChecker.
- Underpriced AI: photo + barcode, comps from "eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook, Etsy,
  Depop + specialists", $4 per 5-scan pack or $5/mo. Own accuracy admission: estimates
  "are looser on antiques, art and pottery, where our own estimates typically fall
  within about 40 to 50% of the sale price".
  https://underpricedai.com/blog/ai-pricing-tools-for-resellers

### Why it matters for clothing (numbers)
- "A used Lululemon Align in good condition often sits around $25-$35 on eBay. The same
  item on Poshmark moves at $40-$60"; "eBay median $30. Poshmark median $52"; "Items
  marked NWT consistently fetch 1.5x to 2.5x the same item without tags".
  https://marketplaceiq.app/blog/poshmark-sold-price-checker
- "Pricing from active listings is one of the biggest mistakes in reselling - sellers
  ask whatever they want; only sold listings show what buyers actually paid"
  (resellchecker). https://www.resellchecker.com/how-to-find-sold-comps

## 12. Kyozou / SellerCloud / Sellbrite (enterprise multichannel)

### Sellbrite
- Channels: Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Walmart, Google Shopping, BigCommerce,
  WooCommerce, Newegg, Sears. No Poshmark, Mercari, Depop.
- Pricing: Free Forever up to 30 orders/mo; Pro 100 $29; Pro 500 $79; Pro 2K $179
  (search summary; vendor page only says "Free to start + unlimited access for 14 days").
  https://www.sellbrite.com/pricing/
- Target: "US-based sellers primarily using Amazon, eBay, and Walmart" (OneCart).

### Descartes Sellercloud
- Usage-based, not public; "most sellers reporting monthly costs starting around
  $1,000/month for smaller operations, scaling to $2,000-5,000+". WMS (Skustack),
  350+ integrations. Acquired by Descartes 2022.
  https://www.getonecart.com/best-multichannel-listing-software/

### Kyozou
- Pricing page 404s; no 2026 pricing found. (unverified) Canadian eBay-centric
  inventory/listing/WMS platform for high-volume stores.

### Takeaway
None of the three touch Poshmark/Mercari/Depop, none do per-item thrift COGS or
mileage, and the entry price is 10-100x a solo clothing reseller's budget. They compete
with FlipDesk only for the 1,000+ SKU eBay-plus-Amazon store.

## 13. QuickBooks / Wave / Everlance for resellers

### QuickBooks
- Self-Employed $15/mo: "No real inventory tracking, no COGS category by default, no
  proper P&L with COGS separated". Online Simple Start $30/mo: "Individual item COGS
  tracking still requires manual entry or a third-party integration".
  https://www.underpriced.app/blog/reseller-accounting-software-quickbooks-vs-alternatives-2026
- eBay forum: bidprice101 "Switched from QuickBooks to Xero (cheapest plan) because
  inventory tracking requires expensive QuickBooks tiers"; sin-n-dex (professional
  bookkeeper) "finds QuickBooks time-consuming; prefers spreadsheets with formulas."
  https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Bookkeeping-and-inventory-software-recommendations/td-p/34872392
- Seller Ledger testimonial: "The only info QuickBooks gets for income is our
  payouts...which is far from an accurate way to track sales data." (Josh W.)
  https://sellerledger.com/reviews/

### Wave
- Free. "No mileage tracking, no inventory management, email-only support, no TurboTax
  integration" (underpriced.app). Fine as a general ledger, useless for per-item COGS.

### Everlance (mileage)
- Basic free: 30 auto-detected trips/mo; Starter $8.99/mo or $69.99/yr unlimited auto
  tracking + bank feed; Professional $19.99/mo or $99.99/yr adds AI deduction finder.
  Claims "drivers save $6,500 a year in tax deductions". https://www.everlance.com/pricing
- Generic gig-worker tool; nothing ties a trip to a sourcing haul or a store.

### Seller Ledger (the tool eBay sellers actually name)
- From the Outright/GoDaddy Bookkeeping founders. Connects Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify,
  Walmart, Poshmark (report upload), Mercari (report upload, no public API), Whatnot,
  PayPal, banks. Auto Schedule C. CSV export added 2025. No Pirate Ship integration
  ("Pirate Ship lacks an API").
- Pricing: $10/mo (250 transactions), $20 (1,000), $50 (5,000), $100 unlimited; 30-day
  trial. https://sellerledger.com/reviews/
- Quotes: "Got rid of QuickBooks this morning.. so happy you are providing accurate
  information from eBay." (Alice C.); "I feel like my life has been given back to me."
  (Alex A., Transient Books).
- Forum friction: knifeaholic asked how returns, relists and partial refunds are handled;
  founder kreeth: "This one I might need to dig in a bit more based on an example."
  knifeaholic then "Noted difficulty finding contact information on the Seller Ledger
  website." mam98031: "Well that would be a GREAT clue that they are NOT the right program
  for you."
  https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Questions-for-those-who-use-Seller-Ledger/td-p/34850278/

### Tax context (2026)
- 1099-K threshold reverted to $20,000 AND 200 transactions (One Big Beautiful Bill Act,
  July 2025). Mileage about 69 cents/mile. Closo guide: "If you mix personal groceries
  and bubble wrap on the same card, you are entering a world of pain during an audit."
  https://closo.co/blogs/legal-compliance/the-ultimate-guide-to-reseller-taxes-2026-what-you-actually-owe
- Worked example (underpriced.app): "$30,000 gross on eBay... COGS was $8,000, shipping
  supplies $2,400, platform fees $3,900, mileage $3,200... taxable profit is around
  $12,500, not $30,000."
- eBay forum, mam98031: "Cash accounting is the method I've used since I started selling
  on line. I have never reported any inventory." and "Pick one and stick with it. Don't go
  back and forth, that will get you in trouble with the IRS."
  https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/How-is-everyone-handling-1099-K-forms-on-taxes/m-p/34337616

## 14. Pirate Ship / Shippo / ShipStation

### Pirate Ship
- "No monthly fees, payment fees, or hidden costs"; "Save up to 87% off USPS & UPS
  rates"; USPS Commercial Pricing, Priority Mail Cubic, Simple Export Rate. USPS + UPS
  only, no public API, spreadsheet batch import. "We've saved shippers $705,000,000.00
  since 2014". https://www.pirateship.com/
- eBay forum pain: carrier weight adjustments passed through without approval ("USPS
  claimed a package weighed 15 lbs... user had weighed it at under 5 lbs... Pirate Ship
  then charged to the user without getting approval"); on the other side "eBay wanted
  $26.36 to ship an item while Discogs and Pirate Ship both charged $17.22" and "eBay
  rounds up to the next pound at 15.999 lbs, whereas Pirate Ship handles exact weights".
  (search summary of community.ebay.com threads; the original thread now 404s, unverified)

### Shippo
- Starter free: 30 labels/mo, then $0.08/label; own carrier account 5 cents/label.
  Pro $17/mo ($205/yr). Premier custom. USPS/UPS/FedEx/DHL discounts, "Unlimited store
  connections" (Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, WooCommerce, etc.). https://goshippo.com/pricing
- Shippo's own comparison admits Pirate Ship is "especially popular among eBay resellers
  and very small online shops".

### ShipStation
- Starter $14.99 (3 users), Standard $29.99 (10 users), Premium $349.99 (15 users);
  shipment tiers 50 / 100 / 500 / 1,000 / up to 100,000 per month. Marketplaces: Etsy,
  eBay, Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop, Shopify, WooCommerce. No Poshmark, Mercari, Depop.
  https://www.shipstation.com/pricing/
- "Smaller businesses often pay for capabilities they don't need" (Shippo blog).

### Clothing-reseller reality
- Poshmark and Mercari issue their own prepaid labels; eBay labels are inside Seller
  Hub. A third-party label tool only helps for eBay (when cheaper), Depop/Grailed/Etsy/
  own-site orders. The unmet bit is not label buying, it is getting the actual label
  cost per item back into the P&L (Seller Ledger: "Pirate Ship lacks an API").

## Unmet needs resellers describe

1. Still in spreadsheets, by choice, because the tools do not fit.
   - "Spreadsheet works for me. I enter everything when I obtain it with the cost I paid.
     When it sells I print up the sale order and enter into spreadsheet at my leisure."
     (shoprosella) https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Recommendations-for-inventory-programs/m-p/33939235
   - bigtexasyardsale stores COGS "in the custom label field (location"x"cost format)"
     on the eBay listing itself so it can be pulled back out of eBay reports. (a
     workaround for eBay having no cost field)
     https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Bookkeeping-and-inventory-software-recommendations/td-p/34872392
   - onefootflipper: "two Google Sheets spreadsheets - one for product purchases, one for
     other expenses - plus a notepad file for consignment tracking." (same thread)
   - A professional bookkeeper (sin-n-dex) "finds QuickBooks time-consuming; prefers
     spreadsheets with formulas." (same thread)
   - The Gumroad/Etsy market for reseller Google Sheets templates (Hustlin Hooks, Flipper
     Tool Kit, "Reseller OS" Notion) is large enough to be its own category.

2. One price change across every platform.
   - vintagecoachrenewal "Inquired about software enabling single price adjustments across
     five sales platforms simultaneously." (same eBay thread)

3. COGS for cash purchases and how returns/relists/partial refunds flow to the P&L.
   - knifeaholic's four questions to Seller Ledger: manual cost for "a $20 yard sale
     item", Pirate Ship costs, CSV export, and "How are returns, relists, and partial
     refunds handled?" The founder could not answer the last one on the spot.
     https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Questions-for-those-who-use-Seller-Ledger/td-p/34850278/
   - MRG con: "no field to enter sourcing location" (theecommercemom).

4. Non-eBay sales are manual everywhere.
   - Flipwise: "Record Amazon, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy or offline sales right alongside
     eBay." = typed in by hand. Closo: "Non-eBay sales need manual entry"; "Ghost
     inventory" risk.
   - Seller Ledger: Mercari and Poshmark are CSV report uploads because "Mercari doesn't
     provide a public API".
   - Vendoo detection misses: "So often it is not able to correctly pull items from
     eBay." (Trustpilot)

5. Shipping cost leakage into the P&L.
   - "I realized I was losing money on heavy winter coats because I wasn't accounting for
     the shipping variance correctly." (Closo Flipwise review)
   - Seller Ledger: "Pirate Ship lacks an API, so integration isn't available; costs
     appear on credit card statements instead."
   - Carrier adjustments billed after the fact without approval (Pirate Ship eBay thread,
     unverified).

6. Death pile / inventory aging.
   - "If you have $5,000 worth of cost basis in unlisted inventory, that's $5,000 you
     can't use to buy better, faster-selling items." and "Sourcing is fun... Listing is
     boring." https://www.underpriced.app/blog/avoiding-death-pile-guide
   - Recommended discipline: "Set a processing capacity - say, 30 items per week. If you
     have 30 or more items waiting to be listed, you don't source until you're under that
     threshold." (same)
   - Flippd users describe the death-pile counter as motivation: "Keeps me motivated."
     (Aj5508). Flipwise sells "inventory aging reports" + auto repricing on relist as the
     cure.

7. The sourcing decision ("should I buy this at $8.99").
   - "When you are standing in the store deciding whether to spend $8.99 on an item,
     knowing your net profit after fees is the number that actually matters."
     https://underpricedai.com/blog/best-thrift-store-apps
   - "You need to know what it sells for before you buy it, not after you have already
     invested time listing it and waiting for a sale that never comes." (same)
   - "The vast majority of valuable thrift store inventory does not have a barcode."
     (same) - which is why every 2025-26 entrant is photo-first.
   - Apparel-specific: eBay-only comps under-price Poshmark categories by 40-70%
     ("eBay median $30. Poshmark median $52"). https://marketplaceiq.app/blog/poshmark-sold-price-checker
   - Authenticity: "it can't tell if something is a replica." (Thrifted review)

8. Tax season panic and the cash-vs-accrual trap.
   - "I stared at that piece of paper with a mix of pride and sheer panic." and "the
     money in my PayPal account felt like 'internet money'" (Closo tax guide)
   - "Cash accounting is the method I've used since I started selling on line. I have
     never reported any inventory." (mam98031) - a large share of hobby-scale sellers
     never track inventory value at all, so a tool that forces accrual loses them.
   - "Using flipwise for taxes, my accountant never saw a first-year business owner do as
     good a job as I did." (Flipwise testimonial) - the emotional payoff a tool can own.

9. Mileage nobody logs.
   - Everlance claims "drivers save $6,500 a year"; Flipwise, MRG (unverified), Flippd
     and SellerHound each bolt on mileage because it is the deduction resellers most
     often skip. None link a trip to the haul bought on it.

10. Price: the ceiling for a solo clothing reseller is about $20/mo.
   - knifeaholic rejected Flipwise at $40/mo; List Perfectly analytics needs $49-69;
     WorthPoint $30-60 is called "steep for casual sellers"; ResellAIO undercuts MRG at a
     flat $10. Seller Ledger's $10-20 and MRG's $9.99-19.99 define the accepted band.

## Cross-cutting takeaways

1. The "all-in-one" does not exist. Every tool is anchored on one job: Flipwise on
   eBay automation, MRG and Seller Ledger on the ledger, Vendoo and LP on crosslisting
   with analytics bolted on, ThriftAI/Cluzy on the aisle decision. Each treats the other
   jobs as manual entry. Cluzy's own line names the gap: "scan in one app, retype the
   listing in another."

2. eBay is the only channel anyone syncs properly. Poshmark and Mercari are CSV uploads
   (Seller Ledger) or hand entry (Flipwise) because there is no public API; Vendoo/LP
   "detect" sales by watching listings and get it wrong often enough to get Poshmark
   users banned. A toolkit that owns the listing on each platform (as FlipDesk does for
   eBay) is structurally the only way to get clean per-platform sales without scraping.

3. Per-item COGS is the atom, and nobody captures it at the point of purchase. Sellers
   hack it into eBay's custom label, type it in when they get home (Flippd's best
   review), or never track inventory at all. The sourcing scanner is the natural place
   to capture cost, location, date and photo in one motion; no scanner app does the
   bookkeeping and no bookkeeping app has a scanner.

4. Sourcing scanners are the 2025-26 growth category and they are cheap ($5/mo) and
   thin: eBay-only comps, no condition input, no replica detection, no hand-off into
   inventory. Their "profit after fees" number ignores condition grade, which is exactly
   the variable GradeThread quantifies.

5. Clothing needs multi-marketplace comps. The Poshmark-vs-eBay spread on activewear,
   premium denim and NWT items (1.5-2.5x) means an eBay-only comp under-prices the
   category. Terapeak, WorthPoint, Keepa and ScanPower are all wrong-shaped for used
   apparel (barcode or antiques).

6. Tax features sell the ledger. Schedule C export, cash-or-accrual toggle, mileage,
   and "my accountant was impressed" are the recurring purchase triggers; the $20K/200
   1099-K threshold (2026) shrinks the number of forced filers, so the pitch shifts from
   compliance fear to "know your real profit".

7. Death pile is a motivational feature, not a report. Users of Flippd and Flipwise talk
   about the counter as a game; aging + auto-reprice-on-relist is the retention hook.

8. Shipping: Pirate Ship (free, USPS+UPS) is the reseller default; ShipStation/Shippo
   are over-built and skip Poshmark/Mercari. The value is not the label, it is the label
   cost and the carrier adjustment landing on the right item in the P&L.

9. Price band: $10-20/mo is where reseller bookkeeping lives; $40+ gets rejected by
   sub-1,000-listing sellers; enterprise (Sellbrite $29-179, Sellercloud $1k+) is a
   different buyer.

10. Naming hazards for the brief: "Reseller Assistant" is a Poshmark VA service;
    "SellerAide" is SellerAider (crosslister); SellHound pivoted to AI product photos in
    2026 and "SellerHound" is an unrelated indie iOS app.
