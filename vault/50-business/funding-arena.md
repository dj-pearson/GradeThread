# GradeThread: funding and strategic-partnership arena

Researched 2026-09-08. Sources fetched directly are listed under each claim
and again in the Sources section. Anything not confirmed from a primary page
is marked "(unverified)". Bing's AI answer box was used to find pages; every
figure below was then checked against the page it cites unless marked.

Context: GradeThread (gradethread.com, Pearson Media LLC) is a one-founder
SaaS doing AI condition grading of pre-owned clothing (1.0-10.0 grade, factor
report, public certificate) plus FlipDesk, a reseller toolkit. Traffic is in
the hundreds of pageviews a month. Goal: be to eBay for clothing what PSA is
to eBay for cards, or pitch Poshmark or another large player, and possibly
raise, starting with eBay Ventures.

## 1. eBay Ventures

### What it is
- Corporate venture arm of eBay Inc., set up in 2022. Team of five. "We are a
  multi-stage investment firm with a global scope. We tend to focus on
  early-stage investments, but we are also open to later-stage investments
  where there is a great fit." (https://www.ebayinc.com/ebay-ventures/,
  read 2026-09-08)
- Global Venturing profile (2025-11-24): "20 investments made, 3 exits";
  focus on "marketplaces, ecommerce enablers, and future of commerce."
  Key quote from Henri Jaanimagi: "We try to move fast, and we've slightly
  loosened the need for commercial partnership as a prerequisite for
  investment." He also says the team works "with various teams within the
  corporate" to find what unlocks a vertical now and 5-10 years out.
  (https://globalventuring.com/corporate/consumer/ebay-using-startup-investment-to-keep-reinventing-itself/)
- Stated 2026 direction: "eBay is directing its venture capital arm toward
  'ecommerce enablers' and AI startups for 2026," backing WunderGraph,
  Refiberd and "AI appraisal platform Vardera"; shifting away from
  category-specific bets like the failed luxury marketplace Cudoni.
  (Shopifreaks, 2025-12-29,
  https://www.shopifreaks.com/ebay-pivots-venture-strategy-to-fund-ai-and-saas-enablers/ ;
  the underlying source article was not reachable, so treat the Cudoni
  detail as (unverified))
- FAQ on the overview page: they do NOT only invest in startups that touch
  the eBay marketplace ("Absolutely not"), and founders need not be US-based.
  "How do I get started?" answer ends "...or message us on LinkedIn."
  (https://www.ebayinc.com/ebay-ventures/)

### Investment themes (from the About page, read 2026-09-08)
Recommerce ("verticalized experiences to extend the lives of pre-loved

GradeThread maps to three of those seven at once: Recommerce, Seller Tools,
Marketplace Fundamentals (trust). That sentence belongs in the first line of
any pitch to them.

### Does eBay Ventures invest pre-traction?

"too early for a direct check, right size for the Fund."

## 2. The PSA-eBay model, and what a clothing-grade partner would need

### How the eBay-Collectors (PSA) deal actually works
- 2024-04-10: eBay and Collectors signed definitive agreements: eBay buys
  Goldin (high-end auction house) from Collectors; PSA buys the eBay vault;
  and the two sign a commercial agreement for "integrated grading services
  available to US customers" and a PSA service that lets customers "list/sell
  cards on eBay at the time of grading." Financial terms not disclosed.
  Jamie Iannone: eBay is "relentlessly focused on providing the best
  inventory and highest level of trust."
  (https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-collectors-enter-into-commercial-agreement-sign-deals-for-acquisition-of-goldin-by-ebay-and-acquisition-of-the-ebay-vault-by-psa/)
- 2024-05-16: deals closed; summer 2024 launch. Features: unified vault
  access; after grading, cards can be sold on eBay, vaulted, or returned;
  PSA app collection management with "real-time value tracking"; eBay
  integrates "real-time transaction data and PSA Population Report data
  directly into collectible listings, providing rarity and grading
  distribution transparency"; an image-based listing tool that scans a
  graded card to auto-populate cert number, set, player, parallel.
  Nat Turner (Collectors CEO): "grade a card with PSA, then utilize the
  vault to either store it securely or make it available to other
  collectors on eBay in an instant."
  (https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ollectors-and-ebay-transactions-close-summer-launch-expected-for-integrated-end-to-end-hobby-experience/)
- Live mechanics today (eBay help page "How the PSA Vault works with
  eBay", id=5342): at checkout a buyer can route an eligible card to the
  PSA Vault; PSA first authenticates and confirms it matches the listing;
  vaulted cards show a vault badge next to the listing photo; every vaulted
  card is "insured, inspected by industry experts, confirmed as accurately
  described in the listing and backed by Authenticity Guarantee"; selling
  from the vault is via PSA consignment, with "listing at the time of
  grading." (https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/trading-cards-listing-tools/using-the-ebay-vault?id=5342)

### What PSA brought
1. A brand buyers already priced in: "PSA 10" moves price, so eBay gained
   conversion, not just a badge.
2. Physical custody (vault) and a physical inspection step, which lets eBay
   extend Authenticity Guarantee to the item.
3. Proprietary data: the Population Report (grade distribution per card) and
   pricing data, which eBay now surfaces in listings. This is the part a
   photo-only grader can copy: a public population/grade-distribution
   dataset per garment type or brand.
4. Scale and a consignment/fulfilment operation on the seller side.

### What eBay's clothing side already does (the gap GradeThread sits in)
- Authenticity Guarantee covers seven categories: "sneakers, handbags,
  watches, jewelry, streetwear, trading cards, and coins." Watches are
  inspected by Stoll & Co. No condition grading of general apparel is
  offered. (https://www.ebay.com/authenticity-guarantee, read 2026-09-08)
- Pre-owned clothing conditions since 2025-02-04: "Pre-owned - Excellent"
  ("like new, previously worn, but with little to no signs of wear"),
  "Pre-owned - Good" ("gently used with light signs of wear and/or visible
  flaws"), "Pre-owned - Fair" ("significantly visible flaws, heavy signs of
  wear, and/or missing or damaged components"); "New with defects" renamed
  "New with imperfections." Existing "Pre-owned" listings auto-mapped to
  Good. eBay's stated reasons: trust ("give your buyers a clear picture"),
  search filters, and "reduce the likelihood of returns." Third-party tools
  get the new options. (https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/resources/seller-updates/2025-january/new-item-conditions ;
  EcommerceBytes 2025-01-27)
  These three buckets are self-declared by the seller. Nobody verifies
  them. That is the exact hole a 1.0-10.0 third-party grade fills.
- 2026 standardization push: apparel and footwear listings with
  non-standard or missing size data "may be blocked or hidden starting
  August 2026" (normalization from June 2026); coin listings must carry a
  Sheldon-scale bucket and, if graded, the grading company and grade
  (enforced from May-July 2026). (developer.ebay.com banner, read
  2026-09-08; valueaddedresource.net 2026-05-18/25,
  https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-standardizes-fashion-sizes-coin-grading/)
  Read: eBay is now willing to make structured condition data mandatory
  per category and to name third-party graders in item specifics (coins).
  Apparel condition is the obvious next field.
- Certilogo (owned since July 2023) is authenticity/digital ID for branded
  apparel, not condition. eBay Ventures also holds Fleek Sort (wholesale
  garment grading) and Vardera (collectibles grading), see Section 1.

### What an analogous clothing-grade partner would need to show eBay
Derived from the PSA deal, the 2025 condition change, and the vendor
checklists in Section 5:
1. A grade buyers respond to. Evidence that listings carrying a GradeThread
   grade convert better, sell for more, or return less than un-graded
   comparables. PSA had decades of this; you need a controlled sample of
   even a few hundred listings.
2. Return-rate reduction on condition-related returns ("item not as
   described"). eBay's own copy for the Feb 2025 change names returns as the
   goal, so this is the metric they already track.
3. Accuracy against human experts: inter-rater agreement, a published
   rubric (the five factors and weights are a start), a golden set, and an
   audit trail per grade. eBay's coin rule shows they want "grading company
   + grade" as structured data, so an API that returns exactly that.
4. A population/price dataset: grade distribution by brand/category and
   grade-vs-price curves. This is the Population Report analogue and is the
   defensible asset.
5. Custody or verification story. PSA physically holds the item. A photo
   grade needs either (a) a "verified by GradeThread" tier where photos are
   captured in-app with liveness/EXIF checks, or (b) an in-network physical
   grading partner for high-value items.
6. Operational readiness: API with an SLA, SOC 2 Type II, indemnity and
   E&O/cyber insurance, GDPR/CCPA data handling (Section 5).
7. A wedge they can integrate cheaply: an item-specific ("GradeThread
   Grade: 8.5") plus a certificate URL, exactly as PSA cert numbers appear
   today, before any deeper integration.

### eBay doors a small vendor can enter today
- eBay Developers Program: free; sandbox and production keys; "Application
  Growth Check" is a free review required "to increase the API call limits
  for your application" and "use restricted APIs in production."
  (https://developer.ebay.com/grow/application-growth-check)
- Developer Loyalty Program (Gold/Silver/Bronze "Solution Provider"
  badges): "currently invite only," tiers "reflect a developer's eBay
  business scale and solution sophistication." Gold gets a dedicated
  partner manager, API betas, Developer Council, joint BD campaigns;
  Gold and Silver get "listing on Solution Provider page in Seller Center"
  and Partner Day invitations. (https://developer.ebay.com/grow/loyalty-program)
- Seller Center "Third party providers" page lists Gold and Silver
  partners (Vendoo, Nifty, Auctiva, SureDone among them). There is no
  self-serve "become a provider" form; placement comes through the Loyalty
  Program above. (https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/ebay-for-business/third-party-providers)
- eBay Partner Network (affiliate): two tracks, "eBay Ambassador" for
  social creators (US/UK) and EPN for API/feed integrations; sign-up at
  partner.ebay.com. Commissions per rate card. (https://partnernetwork.ebay.com/)
  FlipDesk's comps and sold links are a natural EPN fit and a small revenue
  line, but it does not get you in front of the fashion or trust teams.
- eBay Circular Fashion Fund (Section 1): the only eBay program with an
  open application window for a company of GradeThread's age and size.

## 3. Other strategics

Poshmark (Naver)
- Naver completed the acquisition on 2023-01-06; price not stated in the
  Naver release (widely reported as about $1.2B, unverified here). Naver's
  rationale: search, AI recommendation and e-commerce tools applied to
  Poshmark; livestream commerce; re-commerce growth.
  (https://www.navercorp.com/en/media/pressReleasesDetail?seq=31026)

  select luxury brands; run by Poshmark's own team plus unnamed "trusted
  independent partners"; no partnership contact, only the support portal.
  (https://poshmark.com/posh_authenticate) No condition grading product.
- Smart List AI (2025-01-30): AI generates listing details from photos,
  iOS/Android. (https://blog.poshmark.com/introducing-smart-list-ai)
- Naver's venture arm is NAVER D2SF ("we focus on early-stage startups to
  help them grow and create synergies with NAVER"; apply page at
  https://d2sf.naver.com/en/apply). Could not be fetched from this
  session; whether it takes non-Korean applicants is (unverified).
  Startup Intros shows only 5 tracked deals (2026-07-13), so it is small.
- Door: no public BD or partnerships page found. Realistic path is a warm
  intro to Poshmark's Trust and Safety or Seller Experience leads via
  LinkedIn, or via Naver D2SF. Poshmark has no third-party developer
  program; FlipDesk reaches it by browser extension, which Poshmark
  tolerates but does not endorse.

Mercari

Depop (Etsy) and Etsy
- No venture fund or vendor program found for either in this session
  (unverified). Etsy has a public developer API; Depop does not.

ThredUp

  https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-8-5-thredup-inc-stock/)

  (Tracxn/Crunchbase snippets; https://trove.com/) Grades items in its own

Archive

## 4. Investors and programs that fund resale, circular fashion and marketplace tooling at pre-seed/seed

## 5. What a pre-seed investor or a strategic expects to see in 2026

Pre-seed and seed market data
- Carta, State of Pre-Seed Q2 2026 (2026-08-13) and the SAFE cap note
  (2026-08-25): 93% of pre-seed rounds were SAFEs (95% of dollars); $3.19B
  raised across 11,500+ SAFEs on Carta in Q2 2026; average instrument
  $276,000. Median post-money caps by round size: under $250K = $10M;
  $250-499K = $12M; $500-999K = $12.5M; $1-2.4M = $18M; $2.5M+ = $35M.
  (Carta pages returned 403 to this session; figures taken from
  https://startupowl.com/fund/pre-seed-funding/, 2026-08-28, which quotes
  Carta Q2 2026 throughout, and from Bing's excerpt of
  https://carta.com/data/safe-valuation-caps-q2-2026/ ; treat as
  second-hand.)
- What pre-seed investors ask for, per the same summary: team and domain
  expertise; MVP or working demo; "10-15 paying customers" or an
  equivalent signal (waitlist, LOIs, pilots); 12-18 months runway plus a
  15-25% buffer. Seed expects product-market-fit signals and is usually a
  priced round.
- Concrete comparables from this research: SellRaze got into YC F25 at
  $875K ARR and 30% MoM; Vardera raised $4.77M seed in its founding year
  with a working API and named categories; Fleek's Series B cited 12M items
  and 50,000 buyers.
- Translation for GradeThread: an AI grading product at hundreds of
  pageviews and no published customer count is below the pre-seed bar for
  a priced or SAFE round from a fund, but inside the bar for grants
  (Circular Fashion Fund), credits (AWS, NVIDIA), and angels. Ten paying
  reseller accounts plus one marketplace pilot is the number that opens the
  seed conversation.

What strategics ask of a data/trust vendor (checklist evidence)
- GLACIS AI Vendor Due Diligence Checklist (updated 2026-06-01, 50+ items;
  cites EU AI Act 2024/1689, Colorado SB 26-189, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC
  42001): SOC 2 Type II report (scope, period, exceptions); ISO 27001;
  pen-test evidence covering AI components and API; a model card with
  accuracy/precision/recall on relevant datasets and documented failure
  modes; training-data datasheet and legal basis; a customer-data policy
  that forbids training on customer data unless contracted; SLA with uptime
  and P95/P99 latency; "model performance baseline guarantees with defined
  accuracy thresholds and SLA remedies"; version pinning and deprecation
  notice periods; performance warranties; liability caps with carve-outs;
  indemnification for IP and regulatory penalties; audit rights; E&O and
  cyber insurance certificates; an incident plan with "accuracy threshold
  breaches" as an incident class.
  (https://www.glacis.io/guide-ai-vendor-due-diligence)
- eBay-specific signals: the developer program requires an Application
  Growth Check before higher call limits or restricted APIs
  (https://developer.ebay.com/grow/application-growth-check); the coin rule
  requires "grading company and grade" as structured item specifics
  (valueaddedresource.net 2026-05-25); the PSA vault page promises items
  "confirmed as accurately described in the listing" (eBay help id=5342),
  so the accuracy-vs-listing claim is the standard eBay already sells to
  buyers.
- Minimum viable package for a first strategic conversation: an
  accuracy audit (blind human panel vs model on a few hundred garments,
  published), the rubric and weights as a spec, an API with a stated SLA,
  SOC 2 Type I in progress with Type II scheduled, cyber/E&O insurance
  quotes in hand, and a draft indemnity position (cap at fees, carve-out
  for gross negligence).

## 6. Realistic ordering of options for a solo founder at this traction

Short answer: partnership signals first, money second, and use eBay's own
grant as the bridge between them.

1. Now (Sept-Dec 2026): build the proof, not the pitch.
   - Run FlipDesk resellers' graded vs ungraded eBay listings as a study:
     sell-through, price, and item-not-as-described returns. eBay's Feb
     2025 condition copy names all three, so use their words.
   - Publish a small population report (grade distribution by brand and
     category) and an accuracy audit vs human graders. This is the PSA
     Population Report analogue and the thing no other clothing player has
     shown publicly.
   - Join eBay Developers Program properly, pass an Application Growth
     Check, and ship the grade as an item specific plus certificate link
     via FlipDesk. Apply to AWS Activate Founders and NVIDIA Inception (both
     rolling, both free).
   - Register for eBay Partner Network for FlipDesk comps/sold links.

2. January 2027: eBay Circular Fashion Fund.
   - Eligibility fits (US, registered LLC, under 6 years). Frame as
     extending garment life by making pre-owned trustworthy (returns avoided
     = garments not shipped twice = waste). Judges score "commercial
     viability" and "openness to collaboration"; bring the study from step
     1. A finalist slot is $50,000 plus eBay fashion executives (Alexis
     Hoopes' team) in the room; the winner slot is the eBay Ventures check.

3. Parallel, low cost: eBay Ventures pitch form and LinkedIn to Noah
   Chaikof (his brief is AI and marketplaces) with the Vardera framing:
   "Vardera for clothing, on the listing side, already wired to eBay's
   Feb 2025 condition tiers." Expect "come back with traction" unless the
   Fund route lands first. Jaanimagi has said commercial partnership is no
   longer a prerequisite, which means the ask can be investment-first, but
   the team's pattern is to invest where an eBay business unit wants the
   product.

4. After ten-plus paying accounts and one pilot (2027): seed.
   - First calls: ANIMO, Rackhouse and Analog (already in Vardera), then
     YC (SellRaze shows the bar), then Energize/Lightspeed/BCV via the
     Archive returns-reduction thesis. Carta medians say a $500K-$1M SAFE at
     a $12-12.5M cap is the market-rate ask at that point.

5. Poshmark and the others come after eBay, not before.
   - Poshmark has no developer program, no partnerships door and no
     condition product; the only paths are a warm intro or Naver D2SF. Go
     with an eBay result in hand.
   - Archive (peer-to-peer brand resale) is the best non-marketplace
     partner: consumers photograph their own items, so an API grade is a
     direct fit. Trove and ThredUp grade in their own warehouses and are
     customers for data at best.

Pitch angle per audience, based on what each has said publicly
- eBay (fashion, trust, Ventures): "reduce condition-related returns and
  make the Feb 2025 Excellent/Good/Fair tiers verifiable" (eBay's own
  stated goals for the tiers: trust, filters, fewer returns) and "trust at
  the forefront" (Ventures' Marketplace Fundamentals theme). Lead with
  returns, then buyer trust, then seller time.
- eBay Circular Fashion Fund judges: circularity and scale ("scalable,
  accessible and commercially viable", Alexis Hoopes). Lead with garments
  kept in use and returns avoided; commercial traction second.
- Seed funds (ANIMO/Rackhouse/Analog, YC): the Vardera and SellRaze
  pattern: computer-vision grading with an API, a defensible dataset, and
  a growth line. Lead with the accuracy audit and the population dataset.
- Archive/Trove/brands: margin recovery and returns (Energize's Archive
  thesis: "rising returns"). Lead with seller/brand time and return cost.
- Poshmark/Vinted: buyer trust below the luxury threshold. Both only
  authenticate $500+/designer items; everything else is unverified. Lead
  with "trust for the 95% of listings you do not touch."

Which doors are open now vs after traction
- Open now: eBay Developers Program and Growth Check; eBay Partner
  Network; AWS Activate Founders; NVIDIA Inception; Fashion for Good rolling
  intake (weak fit); eBay Ventures pitch form (will likely bounce).
- Open on a calendar: eBay Circular Fashion Fund (Jan-Mar); YC batches;
  LVMH Innovation Award (early-year call, luxury only).
- Open after traction: eBay Developer Loyalty Program (invite only, scale
  based); a direct eBay Ventures check; Poshmark/Naver; Archive commercial
  pilot; seed funds.
