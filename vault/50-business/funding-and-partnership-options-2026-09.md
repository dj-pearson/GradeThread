# Funding and strategic partnership options, September 2026

The question asked on 2026-09-08: is it a good idea to raise, and how does
GradeThread become to eBay what PSA is to eBay for cards, or pitch Poshmark
or another big player. Evidence with URLs and dates is in [[funding-arena]].
Competitive context is [[competitor-landscape-2026-09]].

## 1. The short answer

Not a priced round yet. Partnership signals first, eBay's own grant as the
bridge, a direct check after that. The reasons are the numbers, not the
idea.

**Where GradeThread actually is (PostHog, 90 days to 2026-09-08):**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Unique visitors | 173 |
| Sessions | 372 |
| Mobile app installs | 55 |
| Onboarding started / completed | 58 / 14 |
| People who ran an AI extract | 2 |
| People who recorded a sale | 1 |

A pre-seed investor in 2026 expects an MVP plus roughly 10 to 15 paying
customers or pilots (Carta Q2 2026 via [[funding-arena]] section 5). The
one grading company eBay Ventures backed at seed, Vardera, had an API,
a team of about 14 and marketplace conversations in its founding year. The
product here is further along than most pre-seed decks; the customer count
is not. The work between now and a raise is customers and a study, not
more features.

## 2. What eBay Ventures is, and what the form needs

- Corporate venture arm since 2022, five people, about 20 investments.
  Henri Jaanimagi runs it; Noah Chaikof covers AI and marketplaces. Multi-
  stage, early-stage preference, global, and by their own FAQ they do not
  require the company to touch eBay or be US-based.
- Seven published themes. GradeThread sits in three at once: Recommerce,
  Seller Tools, and Marketplace Fundamentals ("trust at the forefront").
- The 2026 direction is "ecommerce enablers" and AI. Relevant portfolio:
  Vardera ($4.77M seed, Oct 2025, photo-based AI grading and appraisal of
  collectibles, no clothing), Fleek ($25M Series B, July 2026, grades
  garments from images for wholesale bales), Alitheon ($8M, June 2026,
  item fingerprinting), Refiberd ($300K via the Circular Fashion Fund).
  eBay itself bought Certilogo (apparel authentication) in 2023.
- Jaanimagi, Nov 2025: "we've slightly loosened the need for commercial
  partnership as a prerequisite for investment." So the ask can be
  investment-first, but their pattern is still to invest where an eBay
  business unit wants the product.
- Check sizes observed: $300K at the bottom (the Fund winner) to a share of
  $25M at the top. No published range.

## 3. The PSA model, translated

What PSA brought eBay in the 2024 deal: a brand buyers already priced in
("PSA 10" moves price), physical custody through the vault, a proprietary
Population Report that eBay now surfaces in listings, and listing-at-time-
of-grading on the seller side. eBay's side was Goldin, vault badges,
Authenticity Guarantee coverage, and PSA data in listings.

On clothing eBay has none of that. Authenticity Guarantee covers sneakers,
handbags, watches, jewelry, streetwear, cards and coins; general apparel
gets self-declared Excellent/Good/Fair since 2025-02-04, which eBay said it
introduced for trust, filters and fewer returns. In 2026 eBay started making
structured grading data mandatory for coins ("grading company + grade" as
item specifics) and blocking apparel listings with non-standard sizes. The
template for an apparel grade field exists; nobody fills it.

What a clothing-grade partner has to show, in the order eBay will ask:

1. A grade buyers respond to: graded listings sell faster, for more, or
   return less than ungraded comparables. Even a few hundred FlipDesk
   listings is a study.
2. Return reduction on condition-related "not as described" cases, in
   eBay's own words.
3. Accuracy: inter-rater agreement with human graders, the published rubric,
   a golden set, an audit trail per grade. The grading engine already has
   the rubric, the confidence gate and the golden-set machinery.
4. A population and price dataset by brand and category. This is the
   defensible asset and the Population Report analogue; the community
   insights and comp-read workers already collect the raw material.
5. A custody answer. A "verified capture" tier (photos taken in-app with
   EXIF and liveness checks) covers most of it; a physical partner covers
   high-value items.
6. Operational readiness: API with an SLA, SOC 2 Type II, indemnity, E&O and
   cyber insurance, GDPR and CCPA handling.
7. A cheap wedge: "GradeThread Grade: 8.5" plus a certificate URL as item
   specifics, the way PSA cert numbers appear today, before any deeper
   integration. FlipDesk already writes item specifics on publish.

## 5. The order to do it in

1. **Now to December 2026: proof.** Run the graded-versus-ungraded study on
   FlipDesk sellers' eBay listings (sell-through, price, condition returns).
   Publish an accuracy audit against human graders and a first population
   report by brand and category. Pass the Growth Check. Ship the grade as an
   item specific plus certificate link. Apply to AWS Activate and NVIDIA
   Inception. Register for the Partner Network. Get to ten paying accounts.
2. **January 2027: Circular Fashion Fund.** Frame it as garments kept in use
   and shipped once: a return avoided is a garment not shipped twice. Bring
   the study. A finalist slot puts the fashion VP's team in the room; the
   winner slot is the Ventures check.
3. **In parallel, low cost:** the pitch form with the copy above and a
   LinkedIn message to Noah Chaikof, only once the deck and the study exist.
4. **2027, after ten-plus paying accounts and one pilot:** seed, starting
   with Vardera's investors.
5. **Poshmark and the rest come after an eBay result**, with "trust for the
   95% of listings you do not authenticate" as the line.

Pitch angle per audience, from what each has said publicly: eBay wants
fewer condition returns and verifiable tiers; the Fund judges want
circularity and scale; seed funds want the accuracy audit and the dataset;
Archive and brands want return cost and margin; Poshmark and Vinted want
buyer trust below the luxury threshold.

## 6. Watch-outs

- Fleek is on eBay's cap table with a garment-grading model. It grades bales
  for sorting hubs today. If it turns consumer-facing or licenses to eBay,
  the lane closes. The listing-side, buyer-facing grade with a certificate
  is the position to hold and to say out loud.
- Vardera is the shape eBay Ventures already paid for: API-first, photo
  grading, "trust signals on every listing", "condition mismatches". Use
  the comparison; do not pretend it is not there.
- A strategic's diligence list (SOC 2 Type II, model card, accuracy SLAs,
  indemnity, insurance, version pinning) is months of work for one person.
  Start the SOC 2 clock only when a partner asks, and say so in the pitch.
- The Fund excludes sole proprietors. Pearson Media LLC qualifies; check the
  registration date falls inside the 6-month to 6-year window at the January
  2027 open.
