## Next moves worth considering

- Reseller-specific placements beat generic SaaS directories: thrift/flipping YouTube channels,
  reseller Facebook groups, r/Flipping, eBay seller forums, consignment-shop trade blogs.
- The eBay/Poshmark/Mercari certificate badge is itself a link engine. Every public certificate page
  linked from a live listing is a real referral path.
- Guest posts on resale and secondhand-fashion blogs, pitching the published grading rubric as the hook.
