## Descriptions

**Under 100 chars (80):**

**~160 chars (meta length):**
GradeThread gives pre-owned clothing an objective 1.0-10.0 condition grade plus a shareable

**~300 chars:**
GradeThread is the condition-grading standard for pre-owned clothing. Upload garment photos and
get an objective 1.0-10.0 grade scored across five weighted factors, a plain-language tier, and a
public certificate buyers can verify. FlipDesk adds the full reseller workflow: source, catalog,
grade, list, reprice, and reconcile.

**Long (~700 chars):**
GradeThread turns garment photos into two things resellers need: a listing that is ready to
publish, and a condition grade buyers actually trust. Every item is scored against one published
rubric — fabric condition, structural integrity, cosmetic appearance, functional elements, and
odor/cleanliness — on a fixed 1.0-10.0 scale with seven named tiers from NWT to Poor. Each grade
becomes a public certificate with a scannable badge you can drop into eBay, Poshmark, Mercari,
Depop, or Grailed listings, which cuts "not as described" returns. FlipDesk, included in the app,
runs the rest of the flip: source and comp, catalog, photograph, AI-write listings in bulk,
cross-list, reprice on rules, and reconcile payouts and per-item profit. Free plan available.
