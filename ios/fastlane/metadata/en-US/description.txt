FlipDesk by GradeThread is the all-in-one workspace for clothing resellers who want to spend less time on busywork and more time sourcing and selling.

Snap a few photos of a garment and FlipDesk's AI reads the brand, size, color, material, and condition straight off the tag and fabric. Review the suggestions, tweak anything you want, and you have a clean, accurate listing in seconds instead of minutes.

WHY RESELLERS LOVE FLIPDESK

Photo-first intake
Capture front, back, tag, and detail shots with a guided camera, or import straight from your library. Drag photos in from anywhere on iPad.

AI that does the typing
Our vision AI extracts item details and writes listing-ready titles and descriptions. You stay in control — every AI suggestion goes through a quick review before it's saved.

Standardized condition grading
Get a consistent 1-10 condition grade backed by a detailed report and a shareable certificate, so buyers trust what they're getting and you cut down on "not as described" disputes.

List to eBay without leaving the app
Connect your eBay account once, then publish, revise prices and titles, and pull live listing status right inside FlipDesk. Sync keeps everything current both ways.

Sales, payouts, and profit in one place
See what sold today, what payout is on the way, and how each item performed. Import payout CSVs to reconcile the numbers without a spreadsheet.

Built for the way you actually work
- Home-screen widget with today's sales and pending payout at a glance
- Works offline — capture and catalog anywhere, sync when you're back online
- Push notifications for new sales, cleared payouts, and listings that need attention
- Full iPad layout with a three-column workspace
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, and accessibility built in

Whether you flip a few items a week or run a full-time closet, FlipDesk turns sourcing into listings and listings into tracked, profitable sales.
