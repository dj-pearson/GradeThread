FlipDesk by GradeThread is a workspace for clothing resellers: catalog items, grade their condition with AI, and list/sync to eBay.

GETTING IN
The app requires sign-in. Use the demo account in the credentials fields. It is pre-populated with sample inventory, a few listings, and recorded sales/payouts so every tab has content to review. The home-screen widget and Share Extension also work once signed in.

eBay CONNECTION
The demo account is linked to an eBay sandbox seller, so the Marketplaces tab shows a connected account and you can view publish/sync flows without affecting any live eBay listing. No real marketplace transactions occur.

AI ITEM DETECTION
On the Add flow, capturing or importing garment photos triggers our vision AI to suggest item fields. Every suggestion is shown for review before it is saved — nothing is written automatically.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera: capture garment + tag photos and scan SKU barcodes
- Photo Library: import existing garment shots
- Notifications: new-sale / payout / token-expiry alerts (optional; prompted on first Sales-tab visit)

COMMERCE NOTE
The app supports listing items for resale (commercial activity by the seller). All selling happens on the connected marketplace (eBay), not via in-app purchase. Subscription tiers, if shown, are account-level and not required to evaluate core functionality with the demo account.
