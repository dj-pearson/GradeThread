FlipDesk by GradeThread is a workspace for clothing resellers: catalog items, grade their condition with AI, and list/sync to eBay.

GETTING IN
The app requires sign-in. Use the demo account in the credentials fields. It is pre-populated with sample inventory, a few listings, recorded sales/payouts, a completed condition grade with its certificate, and grade credits so every flow can be exercised without payment. The home-screen widget and Share Extension also work once signed in.

eBay CONNECTION
The demo account is linked to an eBay sandbox seller, so the Marketplaces tab shows a connected account and you can view publish/sync flows without affecting any live eBay listing. No real marketplace transactions occur.

AI ITEM DETECTION
On the Add flow, capturing or importing garment photos triggers our vision AI to suggest item fields. Every suggestion is shown for review before it is saved — nothing is written automatically.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Optional auto-renewing subscriptions (Starter / Pro / Business, monthly or yearly) and consumable grade-credit packs are sold via StoreKit in-app purchase. The free tier plus the demo account's preloaded credits are sufficient to evaluate all core functionality — no purchase is required for review. Users who already subscribed on our website (where billing is handled separately) see a "managed on the web" notice on the paywall instead of purchase buttons; this reflects their existing entitlement for our multiplatform service and the app never directs users to purchase outside the App Store.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera: capture garment + tag photos and scan SKU barcodes
- Photo Library: import existing garment shots
- Microphone / Speech Recognition: optional hands-free dictation of item notes
- Face ID: optional app lock the user can enable in Settings
- Notifications: new-sale / payout / token-expiry alerts (optional; prompted on first Money-tab visit)

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings → Delete account. Requires typing "delete" to confirm; removes the account and all associated data server-side.

ENDING A LISTING ON A MARKETPLACE WITHOUT AN API
Some resale marketplaces (Poshmark, Mercari) publish no seller API. For those, GradeThread does not connect to the marketplace at all. Instead the app opens the marketplace's own website in a WKWebView, where the seller signs in as themselves, and helps them complete the form on the page they are looking at.

How to see it: Marketplaces tab -> "Sold elsewhere, still listed" -> tap "End it now" on a row. A web view opens on that listing's page. The first time, it asks the seller to sign in to the marketplace; that sign-in happens on the marketplace's own page inside the web view and GradeThread never sees or stores those credentials. The seller then confirms, and the app clicks through the marketplace's own "end listing" flow while the seller watches.

What the app does not do:
- It does not hold, store, transmit or type a marketplace password. There is no field in this app for one. Nothing about the marketplace session leaves the device.
- It does not act on a schedule, in the background, or while the app is closed. Every run is one tap by the seller on one specific listing, with the web view on screen for the whole run.
- It does not answer a CAPTCHA or any other human verification. If the marketplace asks, the app stops and hands the seller the screen.
- It does not download or execute remote code. All page scripts are in the app binary.

The seller is the account holder operating their own account in a WebKit view, which is why the marketplace's own site and sign-in are shown rather than proxied. Settings has "Clear marketplace sign-ins", and signing out or deleting the account clears them too.

COMMERCE NOTE
The app supports listing items for resale (commercial activity by the seller). Selling on eBay happens on the seller's connected eBay account through eBay's API. On marketplaces with no seller API, nothing is automated on our servers at all - see the section above.
