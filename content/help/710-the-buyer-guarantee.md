The guarantee is a promise about the grade, not about the sale. If a certified
garment arrives materially different from what its certificate said, there is a
route that does not depend on the seller agreeing with you.

## What "materially" means

A difference large enough to change the deal. Not a trivial discrepancy.

That word is doing real work. Condition assessment has a tolerance, and a
garment half a point from its grade is within the ordinary range of a judgement
made from photographs. A garment two points away is not.

## The three situations that qualify

**An undisclosed defect.** A significant flaw, a hole, a stain, a broken zip,
structural damage, that does not appear in the certificate's disclosed defects.

**Condition well below the grade.** Wear materially heavier than the assigned
tier and factor scores represent.

**A different item.** What arrived is not the item the certificate was issued
for.

## What it does not cover

**Fit.** The garment is what it was graded as; it simply does not suit you. That
is what measurements are for before you buy, and what the marketplace's returns
policy is for after.

**Taste.** The colour is not what you hoped, the cut is not flattering.

**Authenticity.** A grade is not an authentication, and a fake in excellent
condition is genuinely a 9. If authenticity is the issue, that is a marketplace
claim rather than this one.

**Damage in transit.** The certificate describes the garment as photographed. A
parcel crushed by a courier is a postal claim.

**Anything not photographed.** The assessment covers what the photos showed,
which is why the photos are on the certificate for you to judge.

## How to claim

From the certificate page, or from
[the guarantee page](/buyer-guarantee). You will need the certificate number,
what you received, and photographs of the actual garment.

The photographs matter more than the description. A claim is decided by
comparing what the certificate assessed against what arrived, and that
comparison needs both sides.

## How it is decided

A human reviews it. They see the original photos, the assigned grade with its
factor breakdown, and your photographs of the garment as received.

The outcome is mediation rather than an automatic refund, and it is deliberately
independent of the seller's opinion. That is the entire value of the mechanism:
a buyer disputing a description with a seller is one person's word against
another's, and a buyer disputing a grade is arguing against a dated public record
that a third party produced.

## Alongside the marketplace

Marketplace protections still apply and are usually the faster route for getting
money back.

The guarantee answers the question the marketplace cannot: was the grade wrong.
That matters for you, and it matters for everybody else, because a grade found to
be materially wrong feeds back into the accuracy figures published on
[the transparency report](/transparency).

## If you are the seller

Your equivalent is a dispute, which is a different mechanism. See
[Disputes and regrades](/help/grading/disputes-and-regrades).

## Photograph it before you do anything else

The moment a graded item arrives looking wrong, photograph it. Before you wear
it, wash it, or message anybody.

A claim is decided by comparing the certificate's photos against photographs of
what arrived, and photographs taken after a wash are evidence about a different
garment. This is the single most common reason a genuine claim becomes hard to
decide.

Shoot the same angles the certificate used. Front, back, and a close-up of
whatever is wrong. That makes the comparison direct rather than approximate.

## What a decided claim does beyond your refund

Every claim that finds a grade was materially wrong feeds the accuracy
measurement published on [the transparency report](/transparency).

That is worth knowing because it changes what filing one means. It is not only a
route to your money back; it is the mechanism by which a published error rate
stays honest. A grading service that never heard about its mistakes would have
nothing to publish.

## Timing

File promptly. Marketplace protection windows are short, and a claim raised
while the marketplace case is still open has more routes available than one
raised months later.
