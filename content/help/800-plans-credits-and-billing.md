Billing lives in the Account area, and there are only three things in it: what
you are on, what you have left, and what you have been charged.

## What you are on

**Free.** Three grades a month, no card.

**Credits.** Bought in packs, spent one per grade, never expire.

**A FlipDesk subscription.** A monthly allowance plus the reseller tooling.

You can hold credits and a subscription at once, and many people do. See
[Plans and credits](/help/getting-started/plans-and-credits) for choosing between
them in the first place.

## How the allowance and credits interact

The **allowance is spent first**, then credits.

That order is deliberate and it is in your favour. The allowance resets each
period whether you used it or not, so anything left in it at the end of the month
is gone. Credits do not expire. Spending the perishable thing first and keeping
the permanent thing back is the arrangement that wastes least.

The practical effect is that credits accumulate quietly during normal months and
absorb the busy one, which is exactly what you want them for.

## When you are charged

**A subscription** renews on the same date each period.

**Credits** are charged when you buy them.

**Grades** are never charged individually. There is no per-grade line item and no
metered bill at the end of the month, which means grading something can never
produce a surprise.

The one thing that is metered is a small set of AI actions inside FlipDesk, and
those say so at the point of use rather than appearing later.

## Watching the allowance

The sidebar shows what is left, and it starts showing it well before you are
near zero.

That is deliberate. The worst possible moment to discover you are out is the
moment you are trying to grade something, so the number is visible while there
is still time to do something about it.

## Running out

Nothing breaks and nothing is deleted. An item waiting on a grade stays exactly
where it is, with its photos, measurements and draft text intact. The grade is
the only step that pauses.

Top up, and it carries on from where it was.

## Invoices

Every charge has an invoice, and they are all in the billing area. Download them
whenever you like; they do not age out.

They carry your business details if you set them, which is worth doing once if
you are claiming the cost against tax. Fixing that retrospectively across a
year's invoices is considerably more work than entering it now.

## Who can see this

Billing is the owner's. Admins run everything else in the workspace and do not
see or change billing, which is the point of separating the roles at all.

If an admin needs an invoice, the owner can download it. If that is happening
often, the two of you probably want to talk about who should own the workspace.

## Metered AI actions

Most of what FlipDesk does costs nothing beyond your plan. A small set of AI
actions are metered, and they say so at the point of use.

Flip mode in the browser extension is the clearest example: it appraises somebody
else's listing and spends a metered action, so it is click-to-run every time and
will never gain an automatic setting.

The rule across the product is the same one. Anything that spends something asks
first, and anything that does not, does not interrupt you.

## Reading the billing page quickly

Three numbers matter and they are the first three shown: the plan, the allowance
remaining this period, and the credit balance.

Everything below that is history. If you are checking whether you can grade the
next twenty items, the answer is the second and third numbers added together.
