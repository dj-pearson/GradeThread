The Rewards page is a scoreboard for the work you already do. You do not have to
do anything new to earn on it. When you catalog, measure, photograph, price, list
and sell an item in FlipDesk, each of those steps adds XP. Grading an item with
full photo coverage adds more.

The page has three tabs. **Standing** shows your Grade Integrity, how long you
have been with us, and your badges. **This season** shows the quarter's goals,
your weekly quests and any rewards you have been given. **Perks and boards**
shows the free cosmetic extras your level has unlocked and the public
leaderboards.

## How you earn XP

Every step of the FlipDesk pipeline earns a small amount the first time an item
reaches it:

- Cataloging an item (a brand or a garment type): 2 XP
- Measuring it: 2 XP
- Photographing it: 3 XP
- Running comps on it: 3 XP
- Drafting a listing: 3 XP
- Publishing a real listing on a marketplace: 8 XP
- Selling it: 15 XP

A step only pays once per item. Moving an item back and forward through the
pipeline does not pay the same step twice.

Some things are worth more because they help buyers trust your grades:

- Grading an item with full photo coverage: 25 XP
- A buyer confirming that what arrived matched the grade: 30 XP
- Filling in item specifics on a listing: 10 XP
- Connecting a marketplace: 40 XP
- Putting a grade badge on a listing: 50 XP

Grading steps only count when the grade was paid for. A free or refunded grade
does not add XP.

### The daily limit

Pipeline steps share a limit of 300 XP a day. It keeps a bulk import of a
thousand old items from turning into a level nobody worked for. If you pass the
limit, your items are still cataloged, listed and sold as normal. They just stop
adding XP until the next day. Grading, confirmations and the other steps above
are not part of this limit.

XP can take a few minutes to appear. We check your pipeline when you open this
page, and again every night.

## What a level gets you

Your level is the headline number on the page. It comes from the most XP you
have ever earned, so it never goes down. Levels are grouped into five ranks:
Thrifter, Picker, Curator, Archivist and Legend.

A level unlocks free cosmetic extras: flair for your profile and frames for the
cards you share. You can see which ones you have, and which are next, on the
Perks and boards tab. A level is never for sale, and no perk costs money.

Some XP milestones come with a real reward as well, such as free grades or a
discount. Those are covered below.

## Seasons and quests

A season is one calendar quarter. Each season has a short list of goals, like
grading a set number of items or publishing listings. The season tab shows how
close you are to each one.

When the quarter ends, the season goals reset and a new season starts. You keep
your level, your badges and every point of XP. Your finished season is saved as
a recap at the bottom of the season tab.

Quests are smaller goals that run for a week or a month. Finish one and it pays
its XP right away. If you finish a weekly quest late in the week and do not come
back until the next one, it is still paid the next time you open the page.

## Using a free grade or discount

When you reach a reward milestone, the reward is added to your account for you.
There is nothing to claim.

- **Free grades** go straight onto your grade balance. Use them the next time
  you start a new submission.
- **A discount on your plan** is applied at checkout the next time you start a
  plan from the Billing page. It waits for you until you use it.
- **A discount on single grades** is applied when you pay for a grade one at a
  time. Some of these have an end date, and we send you a reminder before one
  runs out.

Each reward on the season tab links to the place you use it, and a discount you have already used is marked Used.

## The public leaderboards

The leaderboards rank sellers by XP, by graded items, by best finds and by
signups from shared links, both this week and all time. They are off until you
join. To join, open the Perks and boards tab, pick a public display name, and
choose Join the boards.

Only the name you pick, your rank and your score are shown. Never your email,
your real name or your account details. You can change your name or hide
yourself at any time from the same place. Community challenges on the season tab
use the same setting, so a seller who has not joined is counted but not named.

## If something looks wrong

If your XP looks low, check the daily limit first. If a step you finished is
still missing after a day, contact support with the item and we will look at the
pipeline record for it.
