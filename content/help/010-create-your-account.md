Signing up takes about a minute, and you can grade a garment before you spend
anything. This article covers the whole path from an empty browser to your
first report, and what to do when a step does not behave.

## Sign up

Go to the sign-up page and enter an email address and a password. Use an
address you actually read, because the confirmation link goes there and grading
notifications will follow.

You can also sign in with Apple or Google. Those routes skip the password
entirely, which means one fewer thing to lose. If you start with a password and
later want to use Apple or Google, use the same email address and the accounts
join up rather than splitting in two.

## Confirm your email

We send a confirmation link straight away. Click it and you land back in the
app, signed in.

Two things go wrong here more than anything else.

The first is spam filtering. The confirmation mail comes from an automated
address, and a strict work inbox will sometimes hold it. Look in spam and in
any quarantine your provider runs before assuming it never sent.

The second is an expired link. Confirmation links are deliberately short-lived.
If you clicked one from an hour-old email and got an error, that is why. Ask for
a new link from the sign-in page and use it promptly.

## What you get before you pay

The free plan includes three grades a month. A grade is a full report: a score
on the 1.0 to 10.0 scale, a breakdown across the five factors, and a shareable
certificate. It is not a trimmed-down preview.

Three a month is enough to decide whether the grades match what you would say
yourself, which is the only test that matters. Most people grade two or three
garments they already know well before they trust it on something they do not.

When you need more, there are two shapes. Credits are a one-off purchase and
never expire, which suits occasional selling. A subscription suits steady
volume and unlocks FlipDesk, the reseller side of the product. See
[Plans and credits](/help/getting-started/plans-and-credits) for the difference.

## Set your account up properly

Two settings are worth doing now rather than later.

Add a display name in Account. It appears on any certificate you share, and a
certificate from a named seller reads better to a buyer than one from a blank.

Turn on two-factor authentication if you will be connecting a marketplace.
Your GradeThread account will hold an eBay connection that can create and edit
live listings, which makes it worth more than a typical login.

## What happens next

From the dashboard, Snap to Value gives you a fast read on a single garment.
New Submission is the full path: upload photos, get the report, share the
certificate.

The photos are the whole game. The model can only assess what it can see, so a
dark, folded, background-cluttered photo produces a cautious grade and a low
confidence score. [The photos we need](/help/grading/the-photos-we-need) is
worth five minutes before your first upload; it will save you a regrade.

## If something goes wrong

If sign-in fails with a correct password, the most common cause is that the
email was never confirmed. The app will say so rather than making you guess.

If you signed up with Apple and are being asked for a password, you are on the
password path for an address that has no password set. Use the Apple button
instead.

Anything else, open a ticket from Help in the sidebar. Include the email
address you used, because almost every sign-in question is answerable in one
reply once we can see the account.
