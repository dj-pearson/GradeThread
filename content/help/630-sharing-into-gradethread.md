The share sheet is the second way photos get into GradeThread, and for photos
that already exist it is the better one.

## When sharing beats the camera

**Photos you already took.** You shot everything at the fair on the phone's own
camera and are sorting them out at home.

**Photos from somebody else.** A supplier sent images, or a household member
photographed something.

**One missing shot.** You have three of the four required photos and just
realised the care label is in the camera roll from yesterday.

**From another app.** Anything with a share button can send into GradeThread,
which includes messaging apps and cloud storage.

## How it works

Select the photos wherever they are, press share, and pick GradeThread.

The app opens on an intake screen and asks what to do with them: start a new
item, or add to something that already exists. Pick, confirm, done.

## Adding to an existing item

This is the case worth knowing about, because it is the one that saves a
resubmission.

A grade held for human review is usually held because a photo did not show
something. Rather than starting again, share the missing shot into the existing
item and resubmit. Everything else you entered stays.

The same applies to a listing that needs one more detail photo: share it in
rather than reshooting the set.

## What happens to the order

Photos arrive in the order you selected them. If the order matters, and for
listing photos it does because the first one is the thumbnail, reorder in the app
afterwards rather than fighting the gallery's selection order.

For grading photos the order does not matter, only the coverage.

## Multiple photos

Select several and share the whole selection. That is the normal case, and it is
considerably faster than one at a time.

There is a practical limit on how many a share sheet will carry, which is a
platform behaviour rather than one of ours. If a large selection does not go
through, split it in two.

## Sharing while something else is happening

If you share into the app while a submission is already in progress, the new
photos wait for the intake screen rather than being appended to whatever was
open. That is deliberate: silently adding photos to a submission somebody is
mid-way through building is the sort of surprise that ends in a resubmission.

## What does not work

Sharing a link rather than an image. GradeThread grades photographs, so a URL
has nothing for it to assess.

The exception is the browser extension on a desktop, which reads a listing's own
photos from the page. That is a different feature and it lives in the browser
rather than on the phone. See
[Condition Check on a listing](/help/extension/condition-check-on-a-listing).

## Sharing from a computer

The share sheet is a phone mechanism. On a desktop the equivalent is dragging
files into the upload area, which accepts a whole folder at once.

For photos that are already on a computer, that is the faster route and there is
no reason to send them to a phone first.

## Formats

Ordinary phone photographs work, including the modern high-efficiency formats
Apple and Android use by default. There is nothing to convert and no setting to
change.

Screenshots work but are rarely useful for grading, because a screenshot of a
photograph loses detail the assessment would have read. Share the original.

## If a share does not arrive

Two things to try, in order.

Open the app first and then share. On a phone that has aggressively suspended
the app, the intake screen occasionally needs the app to be awake.

Share fewer at once. A very large selection can exceed what the platform's share
mechanism will carry, and splitting it in two resolves that without anything
being lost.
