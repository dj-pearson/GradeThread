# US-9009 — why the crosslisting page sits on page five

Source: the same Search Console export as `US-9001-VERDICT.md`. Analysed
2026-08-18, before writing anything, because the story asks for exactly that.

## The answer

`/reselling/best-crosslisting-apps` is already the page US-9009 proposes to
build, and it ranks 51st. Writing a second one aimed at the same terms lands in
the same place. Two things are wrong with it, and only one is fixable by
writing more.

## Six URLs, one topic, and the listicle is the only one that loses

| Page | Impressions | Position |
|---|---|---|
| /reselling/best-crosslisting-apps | 203 | **51.5** |
| /blog/mercari-vs-poshmark-crosslisting-when-to-list-same-item-on-both | 72 | 9.9 |
| /blog/multi-platform-crosslisting-without-overselling | 8 | 7.6 |
| /flipdesk/crosslisting | 4 | 10.8 |
| /grading/glossary/cross-listing | 2 | 10.5 |
| /reselling/crosslist-alternative | 1 | 7.0 |

Every crosslisting page GradeThread has ranks in the top 11 except the one
chasing the commercial head term, which sits at 51.5 and carries two thirds of
the cluster's impressions. The queries split on the same line: `cross listing
software` at 58, `cross listing app` at 47, `best cross listing app` at 42 —
against `cross list from mercari to poshmark` at 23 and the task pages above.

## The two problems

**It is a "best apps" list published by one of the apps.** The page ranks List
Perfectly, Vendoo, Crosslist, Flyp and FlipDesk, and FlipDesk is GradeThread's
own product. That is a trust signal a search engine reads directly, and no
amount of editing changes what the page structurally is. Every competitor
outranking it is either independent or an affiliate site with no product of its
own to place.

**It is 944 words.** The comparison pages that rank at 8.5 are 1,220 words, so
length is not this site's general constraint — but a commercial listicle SERP is
where depth is table stakes, and the pages holding the top ten there run three
to five times longer with pricing tables and hands-on detail.

The first problem is the one that matters. Fixing only the second gets a longer
page in the same position.

## What to do instead

**Do not build the "multi channel listing software" landing page this story
asks for.** It is the same page, aimed at the same SERP, from the same domain,
with the same structural disadvantage.

**Split the two jobs onto two page types.** A vendor may not credibly rank
itself in a neutral list, but it may credibly say what its own product does:
that is `/flipdesk/crosslisting`, which sits at 10.8 already on almost no
impressions. Point the commercial intent there and let the listicle stop
pretending to be neutral, or retire it.

**Put the depth where the site already ranks.** The task-intent pages sit at 7
to 11. They have thin impressions because the exact phrasings are low volume,
not because they are losing — the opposite problem from the listicle, and the
cheaper one to fix by covering more task phrasings.

## One number in the story to distrust

US-9009 prices `multi channel listing software` at $34.51 top-of-page from the
Keyword Planner pull. `US-9001-VERDICT.md` already established that three of the
four URLs picked from that same pull as "proven demand" have had zero
impressions in ten weeks. A high bid says advertisers compete there, which is a
reason the organic SERP is hard, not a reason it is winnable. Treat it as a
warning, not a target.
