# GradeThread — Google Ads Setup & Copy

> Practical, ready-to-load Google Ads plan for GradeThread (AI-powered clothing
> condition grading). Built to target real buyer intent and avoid wasting budget
> on broad consumer/marketplace keywords like "sell clothes" / "free clothes".
> Last updated 2026-06-29.

---

## 1. Strategy & reality check (read first)

**Who we target:** resellers and small clothing businesses (eBay / Poshmark /
Mercari sellers, thrift flippers, consignment shops, wholesale graders) who lose
money to *"item not as described"* returns and want defensible, consistent
condition grades. **NOT** consumers trying to sell or get free clothes.

**The catch:** "clothing condition grading" is a category we're partly
*creating*, so the highest-intent keywords have **low search volume**. Therefore:

1. Tight, high-intent keywords are cheap but low-traffic — great ROI, won't
   spend the whole budget.
2. For real volume, also bid on **adjacent reseller-tool intent** (where the
   audience already is) with copy that **self-qualifies** (mentions
   grading/condition so the wrong clicks scroll past).

**Why Google suggested junk:** terms like `5 clothing`, `m clothing`,
`see clothing`, `all clothing` are Broad-match / Keyword-Planner artifacts.
**Do not use Broad match at launch** — it generates that garbage and burns
budget.

**Verdict on Google's suggested list:** reject ~22 of 24. Keep only — and only
under tight control with grading copy — `clothing resellers`, `vintage reseller`.
Everything else (`sell clothes`, `free clothes`, `poshmark selling`,
`customised clothing`, `nwt clothing`, `trust clothing`, etc.) is
marketplace / consumer / manufacturing / brand intent → **block or skip**.

## 2. Account structure (start lean, scale later)

Concentrate spend. Start with **2 campaigns**:

**Campaign A — "Search · Grading" (Search Network only)** → 3 ad groups:
- **AG1 · Condition Grading** (core, high-intent)
- **AG2 · Returns & Condition Reports** (problem-aware)
- **AG3 · Reseller Tools** (adjacent, higher volume, self-qualifying copy)

**Campaign B — "Search · Brand"** → 1 ad group (defend `gradethread`; cheap,
high CTR).

Split AG3 into its own campaign once it proves out and needs separate budget.

```
meaning         definition      reddit
sell my clothes  sell clothes for cash   get paid for clothes
clothing line   start a clothing brand   clothing manufacturer
ai generated clothing   ai fashion design   outfit generator
```

Add negatives at the right **match type** where needed (e.g. exact-negative
`[free clothes]`). Re-mine the Search Terms report weekly and keep appending.

**Also turn OFF, in campaign settings:**
- Display Network & **Search Partners**.
- **Broad match** keywords (phrase/exact only).
- **Auto-apply recommendations** (esp. "add broad match keywords" and
  "use optimized targeting") — Google will otherwise re-add the junk.

## 7. Campaign settings

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Search only (no Display, **no Search Partners** to start) |
| Networks | Google Search only |
| Locations | US (your base; add CA/UK only if served). Target **"Presence: people in your targeted locations"** — not "interest" |
| Languages | English |
| Bidding | **Start with Maximize Clicks + max-CPC cap (~$1.50–$2.50)** OR Manual CPC while conversions accumulate. Switch to **Maximize Conversions → Target CPA** only after ~15–30 conversions/month |
| Budget | Concentrate: **$25–40/day** total, ~70% to Campaign A, rest to Brand. Fund 2 tight ad groups well rather than 4 thin ones |
| Ad rotation | Optimize (default) |
| Match types | Phrase + Exact only |
| Devices | All; review mobile vs desktop after 2 weeks |

## 8. Conversion tracking (set up BEFORE launch)

Spending without conversion tracking is the real money-waste.

1. **Primary conversion:** first grade completed (or paid grade) — the money
   event. Assign a value.
2. **Secondary:** account sign-up, FlipDesk subscription start, lead-form submit.
3. Use **Google Ads conversion tracking + GA4 import**; turn on **Enhanced
   Conversions** (feed hashed first-party email for better matching).
4. Mark only money events as **"Primary"**; set sign-ups to "Secondary" so Smart
   Bidding optimizes to revenue, not vanity signups.

## 9. Landing pages (don't send everyone to the homepage)

Match message → page:
- **AG1** → focused "AI condition grading" page (how it works + sample
  certificate + CTA).
- **AG2** → "stop not-as-described returns" page leading with the returns/dispute
  angle.
- **AG3** → the **FlipDesk / for-resellers** page (workflow + grading +
  cross-listing).
- **Brand** → homepage.

Each LP: one clear CTA above the fold, a sample certificate, the 1.0–10.0 scale
explained, and a trust line ("AI flags pilling, fading, seam wear…"). Page speed
helps Quality Score.

---

## 10. Launch checklist & first-30-days cadence

1. Conversion tracking + Enhanced Conversions verified firing.
2. Negative list applied; Search Partners + Display off; Broad off;
   auto-apply off.
3. 2 RSAs per ad group, all assets attached, Ad Strength "Good/Excellent."
4. **Week 1–2:** check **Search Terms report every 2–3 days**; negate junk, add
   winners. Expect to spend more effort on negatives than anything else early.
5. **Week 2–4:** pause poor-CTR/conv headlines & keywords; let winners breathe.
6. **~30 conversions in:** move bidding to Target CPA; consider splitting AG3
   into its own campaign and testing a small **Performance Max / Demand Gen**
   campaign for awareness (core search volume is thin).

**Realistic expectation:** tight grading keywords are cheap but trickle traffic;
AG3 (reseller tools) carries volume. Keep copy honest about what's actually live
(free-grade offer, marketplace names) and let the Search Terms report steer.
