Welcome to GradeThread

One extension, two jobs: an AI condition read while you shop, and one-click cross-listing when you sell. Pin it to your toolbar (the puzzle-piece menu) so it is one click away, or press Alt+G on any listing.

1

Open a listing

Any clothing listing on a supported resale site. Search pages get a small tag per result, too.

2

Get the read

Click the toolbar icon, or press Alt+G. The photos already on the page are graded 1 to 10.

3

Decide with numbers

Condition, confidence, the gap to what the seller claims, and whether the price fits.

Condition Check while you shop

Open any clothing listing on a supported resale site and click “Get condition read” for an independent AI read of the garment’s condition, before you buy.

  • No account needed to start (reads are quota-capped).
  • Sign in to raise your limit and unlock deeper signals.
  • Pin reads to compare listings side by side, and see your own history with a seller.

Cross-list from your own browser

With an active FlipDesk plan, send a draft from GradeThread straight into a marketplace's new-listing form, prefilled in your own logged-in tab. Review and click List.

  • Queue cross-posts from your phone; this browser runs them, and the popup shows every one.
  • Accept the one-time Lister terms in the popup to enable it.
  • Seller tools appear automatically once you’re signed in as a seller.

Your sessions stay yours

No “cookies” permission and no access to your marketplace accounts. Condition Check sends only the public listing photos already on the page. Lister automation runs entirely on your device: GradeThread never receives your marketplace password or cookies.
